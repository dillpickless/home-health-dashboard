# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Home Health Dashboard is a personal, non-commercial application used by a single household.

## What data is accessed

With the ring owner's explicit OAuth2 consent, the application reads that person's own Oura data through the Oura API v2. This may include sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, SpO2, stress, heart health, workout, session, tag, personal info, and ring configuration data, limited to the scopes approved on the Oura consent screen.

## How data is used

Data is used only to display charts and summaries to the ring owner and the people she chooses to show them to.

## Where data is stored

Data is stored in a local database on a privately owned computer. It is not uploaded to any third-party server.

## Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the ring owner asks for it to be deleted, at which point the local database and stored tokens are removed. Access can also be revoked at any time from the Oura account settings.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to the contact email listed on the application's Oura registration.
